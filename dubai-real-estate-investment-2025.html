Pros & Cons — Honest Assessment

What every investor needs to know before buying in Dubai.

✓ Advantages

  • Zero income tax on rental income
  • Zero capital gains tax on sale
  • 6.9% average gross yield (best of major cities)
  • 10-year Golden Visa at AED 2M+
  • USD-pegged currency (AED) — zero forex risk for USD investors
  • Strong off-plan payment plans (post-handover options)
  • World-class infrastructure and quality of life
  • Fast and transparent title registration at DLD

✗ Risks to Know

  • 4% DLD transfer fee on every transaction
  • Off-plan developer risk (insist on escrow-registered projects)
  • Supply pipeline: 70,000+ units expected by 2027
  • No mortgage interest tax relief (unlike UK)
  • Short-term rental requires DTCM permit (AED 1,520/yr)
  • Service charges can be high (AED 28–55/sqft in premium buildings)
  • Market historically cyclical (corrected 40%+ in 2008, 25%+ in 2014)

How to Buy Property in Dubai — Step by Step

The complete process from search to title deed.

1

Choose your strategy

Yield (JVC, Business Bay) vs appreciation (The Oasis, Dubai Creek Harbour) vs STR (Emaar Beachfront, Downtown). Your goal determines the community, unit type, and budget.

2

Get mortgage pre-approval (if financing)

UAE banks offer 75% LTV for expats on ready properties. Pre-approval takes 3–5 days. Required: 6 months bank statements, salary certificate, Emirates ID (or passport for overseas buyers).

3

Sign MOU (Memorandum of Understanding)

Pay 10% deposit (held by agent or via DLD's No Objection Certificate process). MOU is signed by buyer and seller with completion date (typically 30–60 days for ready, 60+ for off-plan).

4

Get No Objection Certificate (NOC)

Developer issues NOC confirming property is clear of service charge arrears. Takes 5–7 business days. Fee: AED 500–5,000 depending on developer.

5

Transfer at DLD Trustee Office

Buyer, seller (or POA), and bank (if mortgaged) attend. Pay: 4% DLD transfer fee + AED 4,200 mortgage registration + AED 580 admin. Title deed issued same day.

Quick Reference: Transaction Costs in Dubai

What you'll pay beyond the property price.

DLD Transfer Fee

4% of purchase price. Paid at transfer. Split: 2% paid by buyer, 2% by seller (negotiable in practice — buyers often pay full 4%).

Agency Fee

2% of purchase price (standard). Some developers sell direct with 0% agency fee for off-plan. Always negotiate.

Mortgage Registration

AED 4,200 flat + 0.25% of loan value to DLD. Valuation fee: AED 2,500–3,500. Bank processing: 0.5–1% of loan.

Annual Service Charges

AED 12–55/sqft per year depending on community. JVC: ~AED 12. Downtown: ~AED 32. Address Residences: ~AED 46.
