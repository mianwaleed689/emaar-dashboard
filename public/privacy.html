Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026  ·  We take your privacy seriously

DXB Analytics collects minimal data necessary to operate the Platform. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account data: Name, email address, and password (encrypted) when you register
  • Usage data: Pages visited, features used, and session duration for platform improvement
  • Payment data: Billing is processed by Paddle; we do not store card details
  • Portfolio data: Properties and investments you add to your DXB Analytics portfolio
  • Alert preferences: Price alert settings you configure

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Platform
  • To process subscriptions and send billing communications
  • To send weekly market digest emails (if subscribed)
  • To send price alerts you have configured
  • To respond to support requests
  • To detect and prevent fraudulent activity

3. Data Sharing

We share data only with the following trusted service providers necessary to operate the Platform:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication and database storage
  • Paddle: Payment processing and subscription management
  • EmailJS: Transactional email delivery
  • Cloudinary: Image hosting
  • Anthropic: AI market insights generation (no personal data shared)

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

4. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies for authentication sessions and basic analytics. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect Platform functionality.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS), Firebase security rules, and access controls. However, no system is 100% secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt out of marketing emails at any time
  • Export your portfolio data
