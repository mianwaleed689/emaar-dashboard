Common Questions

How do I get access after paying? ⌄
Card payments via Stripe are instant — your account is upgraded automatically as soon as payment goes through. For WhatsApp/bank transfer, activation takes under 5 minutes during business hours (9am–9pm Dubai time).
Can I cancel anytime? ⌄
Yes. You can cancel from your account settings at any time. No contracts, no cancellation fees. Your access continues until the end of the billing period. We also offer a 7-day full refund if you're not satisfied.
How is the data verified? ⌄
Every number on the platform has a named source — Emaar Annual Report, Dubai Land Department (DLD), DXBinteract, Zawya, Knight Frank, and TradingView. The verification date is shown in the dashboard header. We update manually each quarter and for major announcements.
Is this financial advice? ⌄
No. DXB Analytics provides market intelligence and data tools for informational purposes only. Nothing on the platform constitutes financial, investment, or legal advice. Always consult a qualified professional before making investment decisions.
Do you cover developers other than Emaar? ⌄
The Competitors tab covers the Top 30 Dubai developers with sales rankings, units delivered, and market share data. The Stocks tab tracks 30 listed RE companies across DFM, ADX, Tadawul, and LSE. Full multi-developer coverage is planned for 2026.
Can my whole team use one account? ⌄
Pro is for individual use. For team/brokerage access with multiple logins, please contact us for Enterprise pricing — it's designed for teams of 3–20 people.
