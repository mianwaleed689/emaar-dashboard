Section 04
The 228 Developer Strategy
Not every developer gets a full Bloomberg page. Every developer IS in the platform. Four tiers based on market reality.
The Real Picture: 228 is the number of RERA-registered developers. But the market is a pyramid. Top 5 developers = 37.9% of the entire Dubai market. Top 20 = ~68% of market. Bottom 148 = 7.1% combined. You don't need to manually curate all 228. You need to be smart about where to invest effort.
